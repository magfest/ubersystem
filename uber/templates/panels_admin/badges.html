What Link and Add New Badge change
Link attaches this panelist, on every panel under the same name and email, to that badge. The badge gets the Panelist ribbon and can no longer be transferred.
Add New Badge creates a free badge with the Panelist ribbon on the account for the panelist's email, or a new account, and that account gets an email. Panelists who share an email all land on one account.
