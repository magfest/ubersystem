Each room is rendered as a bullet with hotel + type + status + check-in/out. Connector rooms get the explanatory note so the reader understands why they appear separately. When the attendee has zero rooms (e.g. cancel email after the last room cancelled) the block renders nothing - let the caller put the relevant copy in. The filter is deliberately WIDER than Attendee.active_room_assignments (live rows only): cancel/expiry emails must still list the just-cancelled or just-expired room with its status, so CANCELLED and EXPIRED rows are included and only never-materialized rows are dropped. Kept in the template because emails render `app` from fixtures with no controller to precompute it. Attendee-scale boundary: every lottery email includes this block, so a change here reaches every entrant. The card deadline and the secure link are per-room because each lottery run stamps its own `deposit_cutoff_date`; `LotteryApplication.guarantee_deadline` is the earliest across the entry and would understate the later room.
