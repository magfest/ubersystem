Staff Rooming Requirements

The shift warning on the volunteer checklist and shifts page, and the Shift Compliance report, come from two settings that work together:

  1. Which rooms show the shift warning picks whose rooms are checked at all.
  2. Night compliance sets what each night needs.

Someone sees the warning only when both are true:

  • they hold a room that matches the first section, and
  • that room covers a night in the second section whose requirement they don't meet.

Nights that aren't listed in the second section are never checked.

Night compliance

These apply only to the rooms checked above. A room covers each night from check-in up to the night before check-out, so a Tuesday to Thursday stay covers Tuesday and Wednesday.

  • No Requirement: nobody can fail this night.
  • Setup / Teardown: needs at least one shift that overlaps the window. Only the window is read; set both times, since a blank window fails everyone.
  • Core: needs total weighted hours across the whole event at or above Required Hours. Hours on any day count.
