Import Hotel Cancellations

Upload the cancellation report from the hotel (CSV or XLSX). The file must include a confirmation_num column matching RoomAssignment.hotel_confirmation_number, and optionally a cancellation_confirmation_number column with the hotel's internal cancellation record id. Column headers are matched case-insensitively.

Upload first to see a preview. Tick "Apply changes" and re-submit to actually cancel the matched assignments. The cancellation email goes out on the next email tick.
